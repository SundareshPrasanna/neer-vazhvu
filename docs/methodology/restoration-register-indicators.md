# Restoration Register: indicators, bands and ranking

Version 0.1.2, 2026-08-29 (0.1 frozen at M0; 0.1.1 and 0.1.2 record the corrections the first city edition forced, §14 items 6-11). M0 output: the indicator specification the register's first
edition (Chennai, 2026-Q3) will be computed against. Thresholds were calibrated on
the 43 water bodies already measured by the deep-zoom pipeline across eight cities
(`restoration-register-calibration.md`, generated by
`scripts/register_calibrate_bands.py`). Every threshold here is published with the
register; a change to any of them is a new version of this document, never a silent
edit.

The register bands water bodies and ranks them within the unit that funds them. It
describes water bodies and funding opportunities. It never grades an agency, never
names a party, and never publishes a national worst list.

## 1. Conventions

**Bands.** A, B, C, D, E and I, in the Wetland Health Card convention used by the
Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (A best, E worst). I means
insufficient evidence for that indicator on that body, and is a first-class state.
Where an indicator coincides with a Health Card indicator, its thresholds are the
card's, so a register entry can be lifted into a Health Card without translation.

**Tiers.** T1-L, the full catalogue on bodies at or above the trend floor; T1-S, the
small-body set on bodies from about 0.25 ha to the trend floor; T2, the diagnostic
record on the shortlist; T3, verification on funded bodies; T0, unassessed.

**Zones.** The fixed footprint (§2), the 1 km halo (a donut excluding the footprint),
and, where a cascade graph exists, the own catchment and the routed catchment.

**Floors.** Each indicator declares the smallest body it can be computed on. The
calibration confirmed the practical floor for the 30 m record at 100 valid pixels,
which is about 9 ha, not 2 ha: an 8.5 ha body has 94 pixels and reads I. Bodies from
2 ha to 9 ha get their extent indicators from the 10 m record only (§4, C1).

**Seasons.** Every comparison is same season against same season. An indicator not
observable in a quarter (optical under monsoon cloud) is carried forward with the
reason shown; the edition ships regardless.

**Confidence.** High, Medium or Low per indicator and per body (§8), shown beside
every band, used as a tie-breaker in the rank, never to change a band.

## 2. Unit of record

One record per body with a stable `nv_wb_id` and a crosswalk to every other id it
carries (census, wetland atlas, OSM, state atlas, programme, cascade node), each link
tagged with how it was made (exact id, polygon overlap, name and distance, manual).

Three geometries are kept: the legal footprint where one exists (FTL, gazette,
demarcation), the mapped footprint (atlas, OSM, state layer), and the observed
footprint (the maximum water extent in the 30 m record, 1984-2021, clipped to a
buffer of the mapped polygon). **The fixed footprint every encroachment indicator is
computed inside is the union of legal and observed**, never the mapped polygon on its
own, because a polygon redrawn after a fill-in reports the fill-in as zero.

## 3. Indicator catalogue: condition (C)

Three interpretation rules from the calibration and the first city run. Operated
reservoirs (supply role in S6) move with draw-off, so a C1 of B or C on a reservoir
is reported with the operator's gauge where one is published and is not read as
degradation on its own. Vegetation-choked lakes read as extent loss in C1 because
open water is what the classifier sees; C4 is what separates a dried tank from a
choked one, and the entry says which. Marshes and natural wetlands (C4-exempt) carry
C1 as a reported value only; their condition band uses C2, because open water is not
a marsh's extent.

## 8. Confidence

Per indicator, from five checks; the body's confidence is the lowest of its computed
indicators.

| Check | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pixels and scenes | At least 2x the floor; at least 6 clear scenes in the season | At the floor; 3-5 clear scenes | Under the floor (indicator reads I); 1-2 scenes |
| Season match | Same season as the reference | Adjacent season | Different season (band held provisional) |
| Boundary provenance | Legal or survey | Observed | Mapped only |
| Crosswalk | Exact id or polygon overlap | Name and distance | Unmatched |
| Sensor era | Sentinel-2 or Landsat 8/9 | Landsat 7 | Landsat 5, or a splice inside the window |

## 9. From bands to classes, route and rank

**Condition band** = the worse of (the median of the computable C bands; the single
worst C band if two or more C indicators are E).

**Stakes class** High / Medium / Low, **Tractability class** High / Medium / Low /
Unknown, **Urgency class** Rising / Steady / Easing, from the tables above under the
active lens.

**Need class**, by rule:

| Need class | Rule |
|---|---|
| Fund now | Condition D or E; Tractability High; Programme none or proposed |
| Co-fund | Condition D or E; Programme DPR or works underway |
| Intervene early | Condition C; Urgency Rising |
| Design first | Condition D or E; Tractability Low or Unknown |
| Maintain | Condition A or B after a completed programme, or Condition A or B with Stakes High |
| Watch / verify | Programme works underway, or Condition moved a band this edition |
| Steward | Condition A or B, protected, Urgency Steady or Easing |
| Unassessed | Below the presence floor, or crosswalk unresolved |

**Lenses**, published weight sets over the axes (Condition, Urgency, Stakes) with the
Stakes indicators emphasised:

| Lens | Condition | Urgency | Stakes | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restoration need (default) | 0.45 | 0.25 | 0.30 | S1, S2 |
| Flood | 0.30 | 0.25 | 0.45 | S4, S2 |
| Drinking water and recharge | 0.35 | 0.20 | 0.45 | S3, S6, C6 |
| Ecology | 0.40 | 0.20 | 0.40 | S5, C2, C4 |
| Livelihoods | 0.35 | 0.20 | 0.45 | S6, T1 |

**Route**, every channel the body qualifies for, by rule: Ramsar status; notified or
court-listed wetland with peak inundation of 100 ha or more and a listed value (the
national wetland programme's criteria); inside a ULB (the urban mission's water-body
component); rural and 0.4 ha or more (the district pond programme); in an
over-exploited, critical or semi-critical block (the employment guarantee scheme's
water-conservation earmark); a live state scheme; an approved DPR or works underway
(co-fund the gaps: verification, monitoring, renewable operation and maintenance).

**Unit.** A body's ranking unit is the ULB whose ward boundaries it touches; bodies
inside the city's mapping box but outside the ULB are ranked in a separate,
labelled regional unit, never mixed into the city list.

**Rank**, within a funding unit (ULB, district or state):

1. Need class, in the order Fund now, Co-fund, Intervene early, Design first, then
   the rest.
2. Condition band, E before D before C.
3. Need index: the lens-weighted sum of axis scores, with bands mapped A=0, B=1, C=2,
   D=3, E=4 and classes High=2, Medium=1, Low=0.
4. Confidence, High before Low.
5. Fixed-footprint area, descending; the tie is shown.

Small bodies (T1-S) are ranked by the same steps at the cluster: the cascade chain
where a graph exists, the gram panchayat elsewhere. Roll-ups above the unit never
re-rank bodies: a district shows counts per Need class and Condition band, hectares
in D and E, the share Unassessed and its top ten under the active lens; a state shows
the same per district.

## 10. Editions and the change log

An edition is one quarter's register, released and pinned like every other dataset
here, never edited in place. Each body's change log lists the indicator bands that
moved, the direction, the scenes (ids and dates) that moved them, and whether the
move survives the same-season rule. A move resting on a single scene or a season
mismatch is provisional until the next same-season observation.

## 13. Cost classes

The register never estimates a body's cost. A Tier-2 entry names the class of
intervention the evidence supports and what that class has cost elsewhere, by size
class, compiled from published detailed project reports and completed programme
costs. The compilation is not part of version 0.1; until it exists, entries carry the
intervention class without a cost line.

## 14. What the calibration pass changed

Run on the 43 bodies already measured (14 Bengaluru, 7 Chennai, 3 Delhi, 1 Gurugram,
5 Hyderabad, 3 Kolkata, 5 Mumbai, 5 Pune). Full table in
`restoration-register-calibration.md`.

1. **The 1988-1992 baseline was dropped.** Landsat 5 coverage over India in those
   years is sparse enough that the window reads near-zero water on bodies that were
   full (Ulsoor 4%, Najafgarh 0.3%, Kempambudhi 0%). Against it, 35 of 38 bodies
   banded A on extent, Bellandur and Varthur included. The observed high-water
   reference (mean of the five best-observed high years) replaces it. Against that
   reference the two vegetation-choked Bengaluru lakes band E (22% and 14% retained),
   Hesaraghatta D (42%), Sanjay Lake C (61%), Powai and Madivala B, and the restored
   lakes (Jakkur 97%, Ulsoor 98%) and protected catchments (Vihar 96%) band A.
2. **Converted surface needs corroboration.** The 10 m built class inside a footprint
   reads 1-4% on lakes with no structure in them (the bund ring and its road). The
   corroboration rule and the 30 m inner ring remove that; with the rule, 29 of 43
   bodies band A on C3 and the four D bands are the known cases (Pallikaranai 16%,
   Velachery 11%, and two small Bengaluru tanks).
3. **The 30 m floor is 100 pixels, about 9 ha.** Three Bengaluru tanks of 2-9 ha read
   I on the 30 m record; the 10 m record carries C1 for that size class.
4. **Reservoirs and marshes need their own reading.** An operated reservoir at 72%
   retained (Temghar) is draw-down, not degradation; a marsh at 37% (Pallikaranai) is
   a vegetated wetland the open-water classifier undercounts. Hence the reservoir
   gauge rule and the marsh C2 substitution in §3.
5. **Trend classes separate as intended.** Only Bellandur and Varthur read Rising on
   the ten-year slope (-6.1 and -5.4 pp a year); everything else is Steady or Easing.
6. **The 30 m yearly record masks never-water pixels** (first city run, 2026-08-28).
   Read as no-data, that mask made "coverage" measure the never-water share of a
   footprint instead of observation gaps, and pushed every marsh and every footprint
   with dry margins onto the 10 m reference. Never-water is now a valid "not water".
7. **The 10 m annual mode is not an extent.** A seasonal tank that holds water four
   months a year is not water in the annual mode, so mode-based "current extent"
   read the region's eris as lost against the 30 m any-water reference. Current
   extent is now any month with water, which is the 10 m analogue of the 30 m
   seasonal-or-permanent class.
8. **Gridded population carries a no-data value below zero** that summed to negative
   populations on coastal footprints; it is masked before summing.
9. **The two extent instruments disagree by a measurable ratio per body.** On the
   same body and years (2016-2021) the 10 m any-month record reads higher than the
   30 m seasonal-or-permanent class, by a factor that ranged from 0.5 to 2 across
   Chennai. C1 now measures that ratio per body on the overlap and applies it to
   the 30 m reference, and reports it; retained values are capped at 100 for
   banding, with the raw ratio kept.
10. **The bare class is a dry tank bed, not a conversion.** With built-or-bare, 431
    of Chennai's 1,217 screened bodies banded E on C3, most of them seasonal tanks
    whose annual-mode class is their own dry bed. C3 is built only; bare is reported
    as exposed bed.
11. **The 10 m built class bleeds into small footprints, and dry-season vegetation
    is not choke on a dry tank.** On small Chennai tanks the 10 m class read 100%
    built where mapped building footprints covered 4%, and 312 of 386 E bands sat on
    bodies too narrow for the inner ring; 755 of 1,217 bodies banded E on C4 and 324
    of those had no dry-season water at all. Hence the structure-footprint cap on C3 for bodies
    under 10 ha and the applicability gate on C4 (both in §3).

## 15. What the register never does

No dissolved oxygen, BOD, COD or coliform from orbit. No optical quality band without
in-situ calibration. No party named on encroachment: footprints, not ownership. No
agency grade. No national worst list: a rank is a rank within its funding unit under
a named lens. Absence of a band is "not assessed", never "fine".
