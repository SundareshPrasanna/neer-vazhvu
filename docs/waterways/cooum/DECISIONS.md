# Cooum waterway page: editorial constitution and decisions

Waterway 2. Pattern parent: the Buckingham Canal pilot
(docs/waterways/buckingham-canal/DECISIONS.md) - a waterway is data, not
code: one manifest + a data build through the generalized pipelines
(scripts/waterways/cooum.json), never a new component. The canal's
W1-W11 rules (two modes one URL, progressive disclosure, claims-as-data,
attributed-never-averaged, no scorecards, width semantics, licences,
preview-first, works lens) carry over wholesale; this file records only
what is Cooum-specific.

Research base (local-only, gitignored): docs/research/cooum/ - sections
(gov-, wq-, hab-, hyd- prefixed), per-topic vintages files, data/ CSVs,
sources/ primaries, figures/photos. Audience note: the works lens
describes CRRT's own eco-restoration programme and CRRT is an audience -
the government-voice rules apply at maximum strength.

## Decisions

- **C1 Extent (OPEN - Sundaresh to confirm before reach tiling).**
  Recommendation on record: the FULL river as OSM maps it - one way in
  relation 11901885, 62.8 km chained, from the origin junction near
  Satharai to the Bay of Bengal mouth. The research corrected the
  handover's frame: WRD's DPR gives the river 65 km from that origin;
  "72 km" is WRD's distance from the city to Kesavaram anicut, not a
  river length; and "32 km" is the ICRERP restoration reach (Paruthipattu
  check dam to the mouth), which lands at about km 35.5-62.8 of our
  chainage. The upstream half is the story's first act (the river
  engineered into an irrigation carrier), so trimming to the ICRERP reach
  would cut the spine.
- **C2 Chainage.** Zero at the upstream origin, increasing downstream to
  the mouth: the story walks the water's own direction. (The canal ran
  0 at Ennore southward; both pages state their zero explicitly.)
- **C3 Length figures are attributed, never merged.** Our measured
  62.8 km (chained OSM centerline, stated basis and vintage) renders
  beside WRD's 65 km statement; urban lengths exist in at least seven
  boundary-dependent variants and none of them ships without its
  boundary. Catchment: 505.88 sq km (CRRT, current) is the working
  figure; the WRD DPR's internal 139.8 vs 505 conflict is recorded in
  the research, not rendered.
- **C4 Kesavaram anicut is not on the Cooum.** It sits across the
  Kosasthalaiyar (Kallar arm); a regulated arm runs ~5-7.5 km (the same
  WRD DPR calls it Placis Canal at 5 km and Old Bangaru Channel at
  7.5 km) and becomes the Cooum at the surplus junction. Headworks copy
  must keep the geography precise. Its construction date is UNCONFIRMED
  (no primary; secondaries say 1940s) and must not ship with an implied
  date.
- **C5 Banned claims (enforced by the verify gate from the first
  build).** Starter list, each traced in the research sections:
  - The 19,500 / 22,000 capacity figures rendered in cubic metres per
    second (the CMDA primary's column header reads cusecs; the m3/s
    rendering is a ~35x error in wide circulation).
  - "The Cooum's discharge reached 8,552 cusecs in 2015" and any line
    routing Poondi's 3 Dec 2015 release down the Cooum (Poondi is on the
    Kosasthalaiyar; the IIT-M report's parenthetical is an error).
  - "345 mg/L BOD, most polluted river in India" as a current reading
    (a 2021 single-station maximum; the 2024 maximum anywhere on the
    river is 54).
  - "19,817 families" as a Cooum resettlement number (the IRCDUC
    Dec 2023 report never mentions the Cooum).
  - "118 sewage outfalls" as current (Nov 2014 LKS count; the state's
    July 2025 NGT filing records 325) - every outfall count renders with
    its date and basis.
  - The in-platform sewage-inlet dataset attributed to Arappor or dated
    2017 (it is PWD data via Nethaji Mariappan et al., NEPT 2017;
    vintage UNCONFIRMED, not later than Aug 2016; Cooum main channel:
    17 inlets, 10.8 MLD).
- **C6 The live series leads the monitoring story.** The state's monthly
  NGT filings (O.A. 673/2018) carry a TNPCB-sourced Cooum BOD row to
  May 2026; the CCWW annual report (last edition 2022-23) frames as a
  series ready to be resumed, and the NWMP annuals as the long baseline.
  Monitoring-currency ranking per the canal pattern.
- **C7 Basin Bridge handoff.** The canal page and this page hand off to
  each other at the Cooum-canal junction (canal reach 6, km 16.9-19.2 of
  the canal chainage); shared sources are cited identically, never
  duplicated. The Otteri Nullah confluence stations (CPCB drain series)
  belong to this junction's story.
